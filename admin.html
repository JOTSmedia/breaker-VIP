Staff Logins

Front Desk logins can look up and sign up members, add points, redeem free table time, redeem codes (app or register), edit contact details and replace lost cards. Managers can also adjust points, change tiers, close accounts, export, mint cards and manage Front Desk logins. Only the admin can manage manager logins. Switching a login off or resetting its password signs that person out everywhere, immediately.
